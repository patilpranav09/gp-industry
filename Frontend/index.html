Who We Are

Your Trusted Export Partner from India

GP Industries is an agricultural export company dedicated to supplying high-quality farm products from India to global markets. We work closely with experienced farmers and also cultivate crops on our own farm located near the fertile region of the Tapi River, known for its rich soil and favorable farming conditions.

Our Services

Exporting Quality, Delivering Trust.

Agricultural Commodity Export

GP Industries specializes in exporting premium-quality agricultural commodities such as dry grains, pulses, onions, and other agro products. We ensure consistent quality through careful sourcing, grading, and inspection before every shipment.

Sea Freight & Logistics

We provide reliable and efficient sea freight logistics to ensure safe and timely delivery of agricultural commodities to international markets. Our logistics team coordinates every stage of the shipment process — from packaging and documentation to cargo loading and vessel booking — ensuring smooth and hassle-free delivery for our global buyers.

Packaging & Quality Control

We provide secure, hygienic, and durable packaging designed for international trade. Our packaging protects products from moisture, contamination, and damage during long-distance shipping while maintaining quality and freshness. Custom packaging options are also available based on buyer requirements.

Export Documentation & Compliance

Our team is well-versed in export documentation and compliance, ensuring smooth and efficient international trade operations. We carefully prepare and manage all required export documents in accordance with international trade regulations, enabling hassle-free customs clearance and timely delivery of shipments to our global buyers.

Buyer Support & Trade Assistance

We understand the importance of consistent supply for importers and distributors. Through our strong sourcing network and close relationships with farmers and suppliers, we ensures the availability of agricultural commodities throughout the year. Our goal is not only to supply products but also to help our clients maintain steady market availability and business continuity.

Global Supply & Distribution Support

We operate an organized supply chain that supports the smooth movement of agricultural products across international trade routes, ensuring dependable delivery and consistent product availability through efficient coordination with logistics partners and strict quality handling.
