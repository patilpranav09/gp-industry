Our Services

We provide end-to-end export solutions with a strong focus on quality, reliability, and transparency.

Agricultural Commodity Export

GP Industries specializes in exporting premium quality agricultural commodities such as Chickpeas (Kabuli & Desi), Onions, and selected agro products. We ensure consistent quality through careful sourcing, grading, and inspection before every shipment.

Sea Freight & Logistics

We provide reliable sea freight solutions including FCL and LCL shipments. Our logistics team coordinates with shipping lines and port authorities to ensure safe handling and timely dispatch from Mumbai / Nhava Sheva Port.

Packaging & Quality Control

All products are machine-cleaned, graded, and stored in moisture-controlled conditions. We offer professional export packaging in 25kg / 50kg PP bags and ensure every shipment meets export quality standards.

Export Documentation & Compliance

We handle all essential export documentation such as Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin, and Phytosanitary Certificate. Our team ensures compliance with destination country regulations for smooth clearance.

Buyer Support & Trade Assistance

We work closely with importers, wholesalers, and food processing companies by offering transparent communication, flexible shipment options (LCL / FCL), and reliable trade support for long-term business partnerships.

Global Supply & Distribution Support

We serve international markets including the UK, UAE, Middle East, and Africa. Our focus is on consistent supply, seasonal planning, and dependable delivery to help our clients grow their business confidently.
